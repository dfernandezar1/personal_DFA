About me

My research combines high-resolution integral-field spectroscopy (MEGARA/GTC, SITELLE/CFHT, KMOS/VLT) with photoionization models to characterize the physical and chemical properties of ionized gas in nearby galaxies — from individual HII regions to entire discs. A parallel line of work uses HII galaxies as independent tracers to measure the Hubble constant and test the ΛCDM model.

I have been part of the SIGNALS team (CFHT), the JWST Early Release Science program on the Whirlpool Galaxy, and the ESTALLIDOS, BEARD and WEAVE collaborations. In 2025 I was appointed Subaru Support Astronomer.
